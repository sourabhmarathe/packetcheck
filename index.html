Who this is for

PacketCheck is for you if:

  • Your lender sent you a mortgage document request
  • You are not sure what is missing
  • You are worried about slowing down underwriting
  • You want better questions to ask your loan officer
  • Your files are scattered across email, downloads, screenshots, and folders

Why this feels so difficult

The request sounds simple. The paperwork rarely is.

01

The list keeps growing

Lenders may ask for pay stubs, statements, tax forms, identification, property records, and documents tied to your specific situation.

02

The instructions feel unclear

It can be hard to tell which files are current, whether every page is included, or why a document is being requested.

03

Follow-ups add stress

A missing page or unexplained item can lead to another email, another upload, and more worry about slowing things down.

The paperwork can also change depending on whether you are seeking a Loan Estimate, preapproval, submitting an application, responding to underwriting, or preparing to close.

What PacketCheck does

PacketCheck helps you:

  • Sort your mortgage files into lender-style categories
  • See what appears present
  • Spot documents that may be missing, stale, or incomplete
  • Prepare better questions for your lender
  • Generate a plain-English Borrower Packet Checklist

How it works

A simple path from scattered files to next steps.

1

Start with the documents you have

Eventually, PacketCheck should help you work from the files you already have. For now, this page is only testing whether the checklist idea is useful.

2

Answer a few simple questions

Share the basics about your mortgage stage, income type, property, and where your funds are coming from.

3

Get a checklist with next actions

See what appears present, what needs attention, and which questions may be worth asking your lender.

What PacketCheck does not do

PacketCheck is an organization tool, not a loan decision tool.

  • It does not approve or deny loans
  • It does not replace lender instructions or professional advice
  • It does not guarantee a lender will accept a document
  • It helps borrowers prepare for a clearer conversation

A conservative starting point

Built from public checklist guidance.

PacketCheck starts with conservative public borrower-facing guidance, including CFPB and Fannie Mae-style mortgage document checklists, and turns that into a plain-English organization workflow. Exact lender requirements can vary.
